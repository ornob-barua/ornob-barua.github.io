Pneumatic CNC Air Motor

Precision-machined air motor produced using manual lathes, CNC mills, and shop tooling at UCSB.

Problem

Machine and assemble a working pneumatic air motor from engineering drawings, holding tight tolerances so the parts fit and run together.

Method

  • Machined cylindrical components on manual lathes and CNC mills in the UCSB Machine Shop.
  • Finished parts with drill presses, band saws, hand taps, and reamers.

Result / Next Steps

Assembled the complete motor and bench-tested it at low and high RPM air supply.
