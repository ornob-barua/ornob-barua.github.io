Signal Integrity Test Fixture

Designed a modular fixture holding Line and Fabric cards for easy TDR signal integrity testing.

Problem

TDR signal integrity testing needed a fixture that holds two different, heavy Line/Fabric Cards while keeping every test connector reachable.

Method

  • Designed an elevated mini table sized for heavy card loads.
  • Added brackets with press-fit alignment pins for quick card seating.
  • Added a sliding bottom PCB mount for full connector access.
  • Made the design modular for debugging systems that span multiple fixtures, with an ESD-safe 3D-printed handle.
  • Modeled in Creo Parametric using skeleton modeling and sheet metal practices.

Result / Next Steps

The fixture enables reliable TDR debugging of line card and fabric module systems. I generated 20+ component and assembly drawings and sent them to vendors for quotes.
