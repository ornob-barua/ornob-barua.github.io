High-Voltage Thermal Validation Fixture

Heat-resistant fixture designed to hold power and return feeders during high-voltage (>100 V) thermal testing.

Problem

Thermal validation at over 100 V needed a heat-resistant fixture that holds power and return feeders at production spacing.

Method

  • Designed modified copper shorting blocks for the feeders.
  • Built a 2x4 wooden frame matching production system spacing.
  • Designed a 3D-printed divider holding a constant 4 mm gap between feeders.
  • Completed the Creo Parametric assembly and manufacturing drawings.

Result / Next Steps

Designed and fabricated the fixture, which holds the feeders at a constant 4 mm gap for thermal validation.
