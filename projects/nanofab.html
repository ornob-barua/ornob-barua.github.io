Cleanroom Equipment Metrology

Nanofabrication Machine Calibrations

Calibrated thin-film deposition (ALD, ICP-PECVD), fluorinated etching (FICP), and ASML DUV photolithography tools.

Problem

Cleanroom users depend on deposition, etch, and lithography tools performing as expected. Each tool needs to be calibrated and measured to keep it running smoothly.

Method

  • ALD (Oxford Atomic Layer Deposition): Calibrated deposition processes for Al₂O₃ and SiO₂ thin films by correlating cycle count with measured thickness using ellipsometry.
  • Unaxis ICP-PECVD: Executed calibration runs for HDR SiO₂, LDR SiO₂, SiN, and Low Stress SiN, measuring pre/post particle counts, film stress, and thickness using Tencor Flexus, Filmetrics F50, and KLA Surfscan.
  • FICP Etch (Fluorine ICP Etcher): Etched Si and SiO₂ patterns, measuring post-etch depth via step profilometer and analyzing sidewall profiles under SEM.
  • ASML DUV Photolithography: Spun, baked, exposed, and developed test wafers, then used SEM metrology to inspect feature pitch and pillar width across wafer regions.

Results

  • Correlated ALD cycle count with film thickness for Al₂O₃ and SiO₂.
  • Recorded particle counts, film stress, and thickness for four ICP-PECVD films.
  • Calculated selectivity and etch rate for FICP Si and SiO₂ etches.
  • Verified DUV pattern fidelity under SEM, supporting smooth operation for cleanroom users.
