Automated Shoe Aging Testing Rig

Automated mechanical wear rig simulating thousands of steps to accelerate footwear dynamic impact testing.

Problem

Aging a shoe through real wear takes thousands of steps. Footwear testing needed a rig that could speed this up and count the steps reliably.

Method

  • Built a concrete-coated spinning shaft and weighted boom arm to recreate the skidding force of a foot strike.
  • Designed a motor bracket in SolidWorks, machined it, and mounted an induction motor on a leveled wooden chassis.
  • Counted rotations with a reed switch and an Arduino Nano.
  • Added an enclosure with a display and a relay kill switch that stops the motor if motion stalls for more than 5 seconds.

Result / Next Steps

The rig has run over 5 million cycles on 20+ shoes.
