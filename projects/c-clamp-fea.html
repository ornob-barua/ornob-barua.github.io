ME 108 Project 2

C-Clamp FEA & Topology Optimization

Analyzed a 6-inch ductile iron C-clamp and explored mass reduction while preserving the original factor of safety.

Studied the body of a 6-inch C-clamp using finite element analysis, with the goal of reducing mass while maintaining the original factor of safety. The project used a commercially available clamp as a baseline and simplified the threaded region to focus the analysis on the structural body.

Analysis & Optimization

  • Modeled loading and fixed constraints based on established C-clamp FEA methods.
  • Evaluated stress distribution in ductile cast iron under the clamp's rated load.
  • Used topology optimization to identify opportunities for material removal while preserving structural performance.
