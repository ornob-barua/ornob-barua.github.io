MEMS & Cleanroom Manufacturing

Microfluidic Channel Fabrication

Fabricated silicon, silicon dioxide, and glass microfluidic channels using cleanroom microfabrication processes and experimentally characterized channel performance.

Designed and fabricated three different microfluidic channel platforms including silicon, silicon dioxide, and glass devices using semiconductor processing techniques in the UCSB teaching cleanroom.

Fabrication Process

  • Performed thermal oxidation, contact photolithography, wet chemical etching, and wafer processing.
  • Calibrated etch times using profilometry to achieve target channel depths.
  • Verified channel dimensions using optical microscopy, profilometry, and spectrometry.
  • Operated Karl Suss contact aligners and multiple cleanroom metrology tools.

Experimental Validation

  • Characterized completed channels using electrical current monitoring with KCl electrolyte solutions.
  • Analyzed device performance and fabrication yield across multiple channel materials.
  • Generated extensive process characterization figures, fabrication documentation, and data analysis throughout development.
